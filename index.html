Course overview

Exam note sheet: For each exam, you may bring one A4 page, front and back, filled with your own handwritten notes. Check Canvas announcements in case the instruction changes.

By the end of the course, you should be able to

  • Reason carefully and communicate mathematical arguments and proofs.
  • Estimate function growth and classify algorithms by complexity.
  • Use induction, structural induction, recursion, and recursive definitions.
  • Understand graph properties and algorithms on graphs.
  • Perform matrix operations, solve linear systems, and compute eigenvalues and eigenvectors.
  • Explain the mathematical ideas behind Google PageRank.

Use this section for course requirements, grading, and policies. Follow Canvas announcements if a requirement or deadline changes.

Main topics

  • Algorithms, growth of functions, and algorithmic complexity
  • Mathematical induction, strong induction, structural induction, and recursion
  • Graphs, graph representations, connectivity, Euler paths, and Hamilton paths
  • Systems of linear equations and matrix algebra
  • Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and PageRank

Assessment notes

  • Homework is assigned weekly. A missed submission receives zero, and the two lowest homework grades are dropped.
  • Quizzes are assigned roughly every other week and are designed for about 30 minutes. A missed quiz receives zero, and the lowest quiz grade is dropped.
  • Class presentations cover selected interview questions.
  • Both exams are mandatory and use a regular two-hour class period. No makeup exam is offered. If an urgent personal situation may prevent attendance, contact the instructor as early as possible; an unexcused, undocumented absence receives zero.
  • One A4 page, front and back, filled with your own handwritten notes may be used for each exam.
  • The current schedule lists Exam 1 on October 13 and Exam 2 on November 26. The Fall syllabus states that there is no final exam.
